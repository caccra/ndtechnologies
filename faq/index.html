Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services — CCTV, solar, networking, electrical and more. Can't find your answer? Don't hesitate to ask.

General

Working with us.

What services does ND Electronic Technologies provide?

We provide a wide range of security, electrical, networking, telecommunications, renewable energy and technology solutions — including CCTV, alarm systems, electric fencing, access control, solar power, air conditioning, structured cabling, Wi-Fi, fiber optic connectivity, Starlink, fire suppression, carports and car shades.

Do you offer maintenance services?

Yes. We provide maintenance, troubleshooting, repairs, upgrades and technical support for many of the systems we install.

Do you provide emergency support?

Yes. Our technical team is available to respond to urgent service requests — call or WhatsApp us directly.

Do you supply equipment?

Yes. We supply genuine, high-quality products from trusted manufacturers, and can also work with equipment you already own.

Do you offer a site survey before installation?

Yes. For most projects, we recommend a professional, free site assessment before installation — it lets us evaluate your property's layout, existing infrastructure, potential risks and specific requirements before recommending a solution.

Can you integrate different systems together?

Yes. Where technically appropriate, we can integrate multiple systems into one coordinated solution — combining security, networking, power and automation so they work together instead of as separate, disconnected setups.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size, complexity, location, equipment requirements and existing infrastructure of your project. We'll give you a realistic timeline during your consultation or site survey, once we understand the full scope.

How can I request a quotation?

Simply contact us by phone, WhatsApp, email, or through the request form on our website. For most projects we'll arrange a consultation or site assessment first, so your quotation reflects what your property actually needs.

SECURITY & ACCESS

Do you install CCTV cameras for homes and businesses?

Yes. We install CCTV systems for residential, commercial, institutional and industrial properties, and adapt every system to the specific needs of that property.

How do you determine which CCTV system I need?

We look at the size and layout of your property, which areas need monitoring, lighting conditions, the image quality you need, your storage requirements, and whether you need remote viewing — then recommend a system built around those specifics rather than a one-size-fits-all kit.

Can I monitor my CCTV cameras remotely?

Yes — where the system and your network infrastructure support it, we configure CCTV systems for remote viewing from a compatible mobile phone or computer, so you can check in on your property from anywhere.

What types of access control systems do you install?

We install biometric/fingerprint systems, RFID or card access, PIN-code access, door access systems, and attendance management systems — and can integrate any of these into a broader security setup alongside CCTV or alarms.

Can you automate my existing gate?

In many cases, yes. We can assess your existing gate structure and recommend a suitable sliding or swing gate automation solution to fit it.

NETWORKING & CONNECTIVITY

Do you provide network installation and support?

Yes. Our networking services include network installation, wireless deployment, structured cabling, configuration, troubleshooting and ongoing support.

What network installation services do you provide?

Structured cabling, LAN installation, network points, network racks and patch panels, router and switch configuration, Wi-Fi design and installation, wireless access points, network troubleshooting, and network upgrades and maintenance.

Do you provide Starlink installation?

Yes. We provide professional Starlink installation and setup, including equipment installation, positioning, configuration, Wi-Fi setup and network integration where required.

Do you provide internet solutions for remote locations?

Yes. Depending on the location and requirements, we can provide or support connectivity solutions such as Starlink, VSAT satellite internet, microwave links, and wireless networks.

SOLAR & RENEWABLE ENERGY

Can you install solar power systems?

Yes. We provide solar power and renewable energy solutions for homes, businesses and institutions — including installation, batteries, backup power and ongoing maintenance.

How do you determine the right solar system for my property?

We assess your energy consumption, the appliances and equipment you need to run, their operating hours, your backup requirements, available installation space, and any other project-specific considerations — then size a system around your actual usage rather than guesswork.

ELECTRICAL & AIR CONDITIONING

Do you provide electrical installation services?

Yes. Our electrical engineering services cover residential, commercial and institutional applications — including wiring, lighting, power distribution and connecting other systems we install into your electrical supply.

Do you install air conditioning systems?

Yes. We supply, install, service and maintain split and ducted air conditioning systems, after assessing your space and cooling requirements.

FIRE SAFETY

What fire protection services do you provide?

Fire suppression systems, fire detection and alarm solutions, fire extinguishers and fire safety equipment — plus installation, inspection, testing and ongoing maintenance for all of them.

Let's talk about your project — our team is ready to help with new installations, upgrades, or maintenance and support.
