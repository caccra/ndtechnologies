Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information when you use our website, shop or customer account.

Last updated: 9 September 2026

ND Electronic Technologies ("ND Electronic Technologies", "we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting the privacy and security of personal information entrusted to us by our customers, website visitors, business partners, and other individuals who interact with our services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect personal information when you access or use ndelectronictechnologies.com (the "Website"), contact us, request our products or services, or otherwise interact with ND Electronic Technologies.

By using our Website or providing personal information to us, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.

1. About ND Electronic Technologies

ND Electronic Technologies provides integrated technology and engineering solutions, including electronic security systems, CCTV surveillance, access control, alarm systems, electric fencing, gate automation, solar and renewable energy solutions, networking and connectivity, electrical engineering, fire protection systems, air conditioning, carports, and related technical services.

Our commitment to privacy extends to information collected through our Website, business communications, service requests, quotations, project engagements, and customer support activities.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal and business information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Submit an inquiry through our Website
  • Request a quotation or site assessment
  • Request information about our products or services
  • Contact us by telephone, email, or other communication channels
  • Request installation, maintenance, or technical support
  • Enter into a business or service relationship with us
  • Communicate with us concerning an existing project

Depending on the nature of your interaction with us, information may include:

  • Full name
  • Company or organization name
  • Telephone or mobile number
  • Email address
  • Physical or project address
  • Service or project requirements
  • Information relating to equipment or systems
  • Correspondence and communications
  • Other information voluntarily provided by you

We request that you do not submit unnecessary sensitive personal information through general Website forms.

3. Information Collected Automatically

When you access our Website, certain technical information may be collected automatically through standard web technologies. This may include:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type
  • Operating system
  • Date and time of access
  • Pages viewed
  • Referring website
  • General Website usage information

We may use this information to maintain Website security, monitor performance, understand visitor activity, troubleshoot technical problems, and improve our Website and services.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, understand Website usage, remember preferences, and support security. Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit a website.

You may configure your web browser to refuse or delete cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some parts of our Website.

Where third-party services such as analytics, embedded content, or other technologies are used, those third parties may process information in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

5. Purposes for Which We Use Your Information

ND Electronic Technologies may process your information for legitimate business and service-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to inquiries and requests
  • Preparing quotations and proposals
  • Conducting site assessments
  • Providing products and services
  • Planning and delivering installations
  • Providing maintenance and technical support
  • Communicating with customers regarding projects and appointments
  • Managing customer and business relationships
  • Improving our products, services, and Website
  • Maintaining Website and information security
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • Maintaining appropriate business and operational records
  • Complying with applicable legal and regulatory obligations

We will seek to use personal information only for legitimate and relevant purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

6. Disclosure of Personal Information

ND Electronic Technologies does not sell or rent personal information to third parties.

Where reasonably necessary for the operation of our business or delivery of requested services, we may disclose relevant information to:

  • Authorized employees and personnel
  • Service providers and technical contractors
  • Website hosting and technology providers
  • Communication and email service providers
  • Professional advisers and consultants
  • Business partners involved in delivering requested services
  • Government authorities, regulators, or law-enforcement bodies where required by law

We seek to limit information shared with third parties to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

7. Project, Property and Technical Information

Certain ND Electronic Technologies services require us to collect information relating to a customer's property, premises, equipment, infrastructure, or project.

For example, when providing CCTV, access control, solar, networking, electrical, fire protection, fencing, gate automation, air conditioning, or carport services, we may need to collect information necessary to assess and deliver the project. Such information may be used for:

  • Site assessment
  • Technical planning and system design
  • Preparing quotations
  • Installation and commissioning
  • Maintenance and technical support
  • Project management
  • Customer service and record keeping

We take reasonable measures to protect such information against unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or loss.

8. Data Security

We are committed to taking reasonable technical, administrative, and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, alteration, loss, or destruction.

However, no electronic transmission, communication system, website, or method of data storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Accordingly, while we take reasonable precautions to protect information in our possession, we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or through the Website.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including:

  • Providing requested products or services
  • Managing customer relationships
  • Maintaining project records
  • Meeting contractual obligations
  • Resolving disputes
  • Maintaining financial and business records
  • Complying with legal or regulatory requirements
  • Protecting our legitimate business interests

The period for which information is retained may vary depending on its nature and the purpose for which it was collected.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and any relevant limitations, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of personal information where legally permissible
  • Request restriction of certain processing activities
  • Object to certain uses of your information
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Ask questions regarding the manner in which your information is processed

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact details in Section 16.

We may need to take reasonable steps to verify a request before processing it.

11. Marketing Communications

Where permitted by applicable law, ND Electronic Technologies may use contact information provided by you to communicate information about our products, services, company updates, or relevant business offerings.

You may request to stop receiving marketing communications from us at any time.

Requests to unsubscribe from marketing communications will not necessarily affect essential service-related communications, such as quotations, project updates, invoices, technical support, or other communications necessary to fulfil an existing business relationship.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites, platforms, social media pages, or other external services.

ND Electronic Technologies does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, content, or policies of third-party websites.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of external websites before providing them with personal information.

13. Children's Privacy

Our Website and services are primarily intended for businesses, organizations, property owners, institutions, and other customers.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for purposes unrelated to the provision of our services.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. International Data Transfers

Where personal information is processed or stored by service providers located outside Uganda, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that such processing is undertaken in accordance with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ND Electronic Technologies may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, business practices, technology, or applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Any updates will be published on this page, together with the revised "Last Updated" date.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to remain informed about how we protect personal information.
