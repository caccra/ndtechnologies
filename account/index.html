Help & FAQs

Common questions — reach out if you need more.

How do I track my order?

Open the Orders or Inbox tab — status updates (In Progress, Dispatched, Fulfilled) appear as soon as we update them.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders in Kampala are delivered within 1–3 working days after payment or dispatch confirmation. We'll contact you on the phone number attached to your order to arrange a time.

What payment methods do you accept?

Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), Card, or Cash on Delivery.

Can I return or exchange a product?

Get in touch with your order reference and we'll sort it out — see contact options below.
