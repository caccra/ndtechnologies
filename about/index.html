About ND Electronic Technologies

Every system,
designed to work together.

At ND Electronic Technologies, we believe technology should make life safer, smarter, and more efficient.

With years of industry experience, we provide professional installation, maintenance, and support services across security systems, electrical engineering, renewable energy, networking, telecommunications, and automation.

Our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction has earned us the trust of homeowners, businesses, educational institutions, government agencies, and commercial organizations.

Every project is completed with precision, professionalism, and adherence to international safety standards. We're based in Lukuli, Nanganda, serving homes, businesses and institutions across Kampala and beyond — every project starts with a free site visit, so your system is designed around the way your property is actually used.
