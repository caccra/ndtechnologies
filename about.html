About ND Electronic Technologies

Every system,
designed to work together.

ND Electronic Technologies Ltd is based in Lukuli, Nanganda, providing integrated security, energy, structural, safety and connectivity solutions to homes, businesses and institutions in and around Kampala.

From CCTV and solar power to carports, fire suppression, networking, internet connectivity, air conditioning and electrical works, we design each installation to work together on your property — not as disconnected add-ons from different contractors.

Every project starts with a free site visit, so your system is designed around the way your property is actually used, not a generic package.
