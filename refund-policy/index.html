Refund & Cancellation Policy

What to expect if you need to cancel an order, return a product, or request a refund.

Last updated: 8 September 2026

1. Cancelling a shop order

If your order is still pending (not yet dispatched), you can cancel it yourself from the Orders tab in your Account dashboard, or by calling/WhatsApping us at 0704 399665. Once an order has moved past pending — dispatched or fulfilled — it can no longer be self-cancelled online; contact us instead and we'll sort out a return under Section 2.

2. Returns on shop products

If a product arrives damaged, faulty, or not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with your order reference and a description or photo of the issue. Once we've verified the problem, we'll offer a repair, replacement, or refund — whichever fits the situation best.

To keep this fair for everyone, returns need:

  • The item in the condition it was delivered in (unused, with original packaging where possible), unless the return is because it arrived damaged or faulty.
  • Your order reference or proof of purchase.
  • A report made within the 7-day window above.

Change-of-mind returns (you simply no longer want the item, with nothing wrong with it) are handled case by case — get in touch and we'll see what we can do, but they aren't guaranteed the way a faulty-item return is.

3. Installed systems (carports, fencing, CCTV, solar, electrical work, etc.)

Installation services are different from a shop return — once a system has been installed on your property, it isn't something we can simply "take back." Instead:

  • Any workmanship or equipment issue found after installation is handled under the warranty terms agreed in your project quotation, or the relevant manufacturer's warranty for the equipment itself.
  • If you need to cancel or postpone a booked installation, contact us as early as possible — before work begins, this is generally straightforward; once materials have been bought or work has started for your specific project, costs already incurred may not be refundable.

4. How refunds are paid

Approved refunds for Cash on Delivery orders are paid by bank transfer or mobile money. Refunds are processed manually by our team once approved, and we'll confirm the amount and method with you directly. Your order's refund status and amount, once processed, will also show against that order in your Account dashboard.

5. What isn't covered

  • Damage caused after delivery through misuse, accident, or unauthorized modification.
  • Products returned without a valid order reference or proof of purchase.
  • Change-of-mind requests made well after delivery, or after an installed system is already in use.

6. Delivery issues

If a delivery doesn't arrive, arrives to the wrong address due to an error on our part, or arrives significantly late, contact us and we'll investigate and make it right — whether that's a re-delivery, a partial adjustment, or a refund, depending on what happened.

7. Contact us about a refund

Email sales@ndelectronictechnologies.com or call/WhatsApp 0704 399665 with your order reference, and we'll take it from there. See also our Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

This policy is written in plain language to describe how we actually handle cancellations and refunds, and is not a substitute for formal legal advice.
