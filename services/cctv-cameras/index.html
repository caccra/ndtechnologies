CCTV Cameras & Video Surveillance in Kampala

HD and 4K camera systems designed around your property's layout, with remote viewing from anywhere — installed by our team across Kampala and the wider Uganda region.

Overview

A camera system is only as good as its design. We start by walking your property to identify entry points, blind spots and the areas that matter most — then design a camera layout around that, rather than fitting a generic package.

Systems are built around HD and 4K IP or analog cameras, recorded to an NVR or DVR on-site, with remote viewing set up on your phone so you can check in from anywhere.

What's Included

  • HD / 4K IP & analog camera systems, indoors and outdoors
  • Remote mobile & web viewing, set up on your own devices
  • Night vision & motion-triggered detection
  • NVR / DVR setup with on-site recording and storage
  • Perimeter and indoor coverage designed to your layout
  • Cabling and power run neatly, integrated with your network
  • PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) cameras for wide, controllable coverage
  • Wireless CCTV systems where cabling isn't practical

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you install CCTV cameras in Kampala and other parts of Uganda?

Yes. We're based in Lukuli, Nanganda in Kampala and install CCTV systems across Kampala, Makindye, Wakiso, Mukono and Entebbe Road. For sites further out in Uganda, get in touch and we'll discuss coverage.

How much does CCTV camera installation cost in Uganda?

Cost depends on the number of cameras, resolution (HD vs 4K), cabling distance and storage needs. Request a free site survey and we'll give you a tailored quote before you commit to anything.

Can I view my CCTV cameras remotely on my phone?

Yes. Every system we install includes remote mobile and web viewing set up on your own devices, so you can check your property from anywhere.

Do you supply the cameras, or just install them?

We supply and install the full system — cameras, NVR/DVR, cabling and power — and can also service or troubleshoot CCTV systems installed by others.
