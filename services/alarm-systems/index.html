Alarm System Installation

An immediate, audible response the moment a door, window or zone is triggered.

Overview

Cameras record what happened — an alarm system is what actually responds while it is happening. We design a zone layout around your property's real entry points, then install a panel your household or staff can use without training.

Systems can run standalone with an audible siren, or integrate with your CCTV and access control so an alert, a camera and a locked door all work together.

What's Included

  • Door & window contact sensors
  • Motion detectors for indoor zones
  • Control panel & keypad installation
  • Audible siren & silent alert options
  • Integration with CCTV and access control
  • Testing, handover training & maintenance
