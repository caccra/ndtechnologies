Electrical Engineering

Wiring, installations and electrical works carried out to a safe, professional standard.

Overview

Electrical work underlies almost everything else we install — cameras, solar systems, AC units and networking all need wiring done properly and safely. We handle general residential and commercial electrical work, not just what supports our other systems.

This includes new wiring, fault-finding on existing installations, and distribution board work — done to a standard that integrates cleanly with backup power and solar if you have it.

What's Included

  • Residential & commercial wiring
  • Electrical installation & fault-finding
  • Distribution board & panel work
  • Circuit work for AC, solar & backup power integration
  • Safety-focused installation and testing
