Internet & Connectivity Solutions

Satellite, fiber and wireless links for properties that need fast, reliable internet anywhere.

Overview

Not every site can get reliable fiber to the door. We install whichever connectivity option fits your location — satellite (Starlink or VSAT), microwave point-to-point links, fiber splicing and termination, or wireless deployment — and configure it to work with your existing network.

This is often paired with our Network Installation & Support service so your incoming internet link and your internal Wi-Fi/LAN are set up as one system.

What's Included

  • Starlink installation & setup
  • Fiber optic splicing & termination
  • Microwave internet links for point-to-point connectivity
  • VSAT satellite internet installation
  • Wireless network deployment
  • Configuration to work with your existing office or home network

Connectivity Options

Starlink Installation & Setup

Professional installation of your Starlink kit, including equipment setup, signal optimization, network configuration and Wi-Fi integration with your existing devices.

Fiber Optic Splicing & Termination

Fiber installation, fusion splicing, termination and network testing for high-speed, low-latency connectivity, with ongoing maintenance available.

VSAT Satellite Internet

VSAT installation, configuration and maintenance for reliable connectivity at remote sites where other options aren't available, sized for business use.

Microwave Internet Links

Point-to-point and point-to-multipoint wireless links, suited to corporate networks, schools, government institutions and internet service providers.
