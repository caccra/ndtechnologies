Electric & Laser Fencing

Perimeter deterrent along your existing wall or fence line, energized and wired to standard.

Overview

A wall or perimeter fence is only as secure as its top edge. Electric and laser wire fencing adds a real deterrent along the boundary, without changing how the property looks day to day.

We design the strand layout to your wall or fence line, install the energizer and earthing to standard, and can link the system to an alarm so a breach triggers an alert rather than going unnoticed.

What's Included

  • Electric fence strand installation on walls & perimeters
  • Laser wire fencing for boundary detection
  • Energizer unit supply & installation
  • Proper earthing & warning signage
  • Integration with your alarm system for triggered alerts
  • Fence upgrades for existing, outdated installations
  • Servicing & fault-finding on existing electric fences
