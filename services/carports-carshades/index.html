Carshades & Carports in Kampala

Steel-frame carports and shade structures built to size — and ready to carry a solar array if you need one. Installed across Kampala and the wider Uganda region.

Overview

A carport protects vehicles from sun and rain, and doubles as usable roof space. We build steel-frame structures sized to your parking area — from a single-car shade at a home to multi-bay structures for office or fleet parking.

Where it makes sense, the same roof can be reinforced to carry a solar panel array, combining two of our services into one structure.

What's Included

  • Steel-frame carport & shade structure fabrication
  • Weatherproof roofing sheets
  • Custom sizing for homes, offices & vehicle fleets
  • Durable, powder-coated finishes
  • Structural reinforcement for solar panel mounting, on request
  • Foundation and installation on-site

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you build carports and carshades in Kampala and across Uganda?

Yes. We design and install steel-frame carports and shade structures for homes, offices and fleets across Kampala, Makindye, Wakiso, Mukono, Entebbe Road and other parts of Uganda on request.

Can a carport be built to carry solar panels?

Yes. Where it makes sense, we reinforce the roof structure so it can carry a solar panel array, combining a carport and a solar installation into one structure.

How is a carport or carshade priced?

Pricing depends on the size, number of bays and finish. Request a free site survey and we'll size the structure to your parking area and provide a quote.

What finishes do your carports come in?

Structures are built from steel with weatherproof roofing sheets and a durable, powder-coated finish.
